# Code of Conduct

Open Reality View adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, events) and to anyone representing the project.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, criticize ideas rather than people, and do not harass anyone.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **anqing.jiang@cn.bosch.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines and may remove contributions or ban contributors who violate this code.
